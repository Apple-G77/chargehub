About ChargeHub

A campus-based, pay-per-use charging service for Cebu Eastern College students.

Mission

Help CEC students charge their phones safely and quickly between classes, without hunting for an outlet.

The problem

Most surveyed students said their battery runs low often during the school day, and outlets are hard to find. Borrowing chargers, carrying power banks, or waiting until home are all unreliable.

Our USP

An affordable price (about ₱5/hr), visible phone security (locked bag, logbook, claim number, condition check), and the choice to wait or leave. No power bank needed.

How it works

1. Check in

Choose to wait or to leave your phone.

2. Condition check

We note the condition of your phone.

3. Claim number

You get a unique number and a logbook entry.

4. Charge

Your phone is plugged into a numbered slot.

5. Claim

Show your number and pick up your phone.

Who we serve

CEC students who stay on campus 5 or more hours a day. The pilot starts in the IT building, then expands to BSED, BSHM and BSCRIM.

Phone security

A lockable charging bag, a logbook, a unique claim number, and a phone condition check at drop-off and pick-up.
